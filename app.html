How to reuse for any app

1. Rename App Portal and the sidebar labels.

2. Change Type options (Ticket / Change / Asset / Document) to your domain.

3. Keep Users, Roles, Profile, notifications, and the shell as-is.

4. Add only the extra fields your type needs. Do not invent a second nav strip.
